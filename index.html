Her Story

A journey of vision, ambition & leadership.

From a young age, Dr Ngobeni dreamed of becoming a leader. Her passion for leadership was evident long before she entered the professional world. From her early years, she embraced opportunities to serve and lead others, developing a strong sense of responsibility, discipline, and service.

Her leadership journey extended through both her church and university years, where she actively served in leadership roles. These formative experiences provided a strong foundation for the leadership qualities she would later demonstrate throughout her professional career — the importance of serving others, working with people, taking responsibility, and leading with integrity.

Today, Dr Lerato Ngobeni (née Molalatladi) is an accomplished professional whose multidisciplinary experience spans engineering, water and sanitation, municipal administration, infrastructure, water treatment, municipal finance, supply chain management, business management, and operations.

"Her story is one of vision, ambition, perseverance, lifelong learning, leadership, and service."
Her Journey

Chapters that made her.

From a Bachelor of Science in Water and Sanitation to a Doctor of Philosophy in Business Management — a continuous journey of learning, leadership, and service across engineering, water treatment, and municipal government.

2001

A New Chapter Begins

Enrols at the University of the North (now the University of Limpopo) to pursue a Bachelor of Science in Water and Sanitation.

2003

First Milestone

Completes her BSc in Water and Sanitation, laying the technical foundation for a career in the water sector.

2004

Into the Profession

Appointed FET Trainer at the National Water and Sanitation Institute, University of Limpopo — contributing to skills development and knowledge transfer in the sector.

2005–2024

Municipal Experience

Joins Kungwini Local Municipality in 2005, becoming one of the youngest managers in the municipality. She is later headhunted by Delmas Municipality as Engineering Manager — during her tenure, an outbreak of typhoid fever struck the municipality, and she led the water-quality compliance response, also earning a Certificate in Management for the Engineering Industry through Intec College. She is subsequently headhunted by Moqhaka Municipality to serve as Manager: Water and Sanitation until 2024, overseeing water and sanitation services, infrastructure, operations, and municipal service delivery, before joining Matjhabeng Municipality in 2024 as Manager: Civil Engineering.

2013

Into Water Treatment

Joins NCP Chlorochem as a Water Treatment Specialist, further developing her expertise in water treatment and technical processes. She goes on to serve as Head of Department at Moses Kotane Municipality.

2014

Advancing Her Qualifications

Completes a BSc Honours in Water Utilisation at the University of Pretoria, strengthening her technical expertise in the water sector. She is then awarded a bursary by the Development Bank of Southern Africa (DBSA) and the Pan-African Parliament to pursue a Master’s in Public Administration and Public Infrastructure.

2017

Municipal Finance Expertise

Continues her professional development at the University of the Witwatersrand (Wits) in Municipal Finance and Supply Chain Management.

2024

Continues Her Academic Journey

Enrols for a Doctor of Philosophy in Business Management — researching how to enhance water supply and address the challenges facing the water sector.

Silulumanzi

A New Frontier in Operations

Joins Silulumanzi South Africa Water Works as Senior Manager: Operations — another significant step in a career spanning engineering, water treatment, municipal infrastructure, and operations.

2026

Her Golden Chapter

Twenty-two years of building. Still learning. Still leading. Celebrating a doctoral milestone surrounded by family, friends, and community.
